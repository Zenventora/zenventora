FAQ

Frequently asked questions about E-menu.

What is E-menu?

E-menu is a digital menu customers open on their phone by scanning a QR code.

Is E-menu free for restaurants and cafés?

Yes. E-menu has a Free plan for creating a live QR menu, adding menu details, managing availability and sharing a permanent QR code.

Can I update menu items during the day?

Yes. Use the menu editor and Today’s Menu controls to update information and availability.

Do customers need an app?

No. They open the live menu in their normal phone browser.

Can I add my business contact and social details?

Yes. E-menu supports business name, location, tagline, phone, WhatsApp, Instagram and Facebook details.

Will my QR code change when I update the menu?

No. Customers can keep using the same permanent QR code while you update the live menu behind it.

Is E-menu useful for small restaurants and cafés?

Yes. E-menu is designed for restaurants, cafés, hotels and food businesses that want a simple mobile-first digital menu and a permanent QR code.

Can customers use the QR menu without installing an app?

Yes. Customers scan the QR code and open the live menu in their normal mobile browser.

Can I upload my existing menu files?

Yes. The file-upload path is for businesses that already have menu files or menu images. It does not require template selection or the item-based style preview, and Today’s Menu controls are not part of that file-upload flow. You can remove, replace or add uploaded files from the menu workspace.
